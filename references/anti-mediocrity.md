# Anti-mediocrity gate

Use this gate during direction selection, after the first proof, and after a major revision. It exists to stop a polished generic solution before it expands into a full system.

## Hard rejection tests

Reject or return to the taste thesis when any of these fail without a named exception:

### Brand removal

After removing the logo, brand name, and explanatory copy, is there still an authored object, behavior, material, composition, or rhythm that identifies the direction?

### Substitution

Would the direction still work if the brand, product, or campaign were replaced by three unrelated competitors? If yes, the direction has not earned brand ownership.

### Stock implementation

Could the idea be reproduced by a default component library, a generic landing-page template, or a standard video transition pack without changing the premise? If yes, identify the missing authored mechanism.

### First-view memory

At thumbnail size, actual viewport, or the first 1.5–3 seconds, where does attention go first? What exact image, object, relationship, or action remains after the viewer looks away?

### Signature survival

Does the signature remain visible across the first view, a middle state, and the ending or handoff? A signature that appears once is decoration, not a system.

### Hierarchy

Can a stranger name the dominant, supporting, and quiet layers without reading the rationale? Equal emphasis everywhere is a failure of direction.

### Removal

What 20–30% of the elements can be removed? If nothing can be removed, the direction has probably not made a choice.

### Polish cannot rescue premise

Do not use shadows, gradients, particles, motion, or micro-interactions to conceal a weak premise, weak composition, or missing main actor. Return to the thesis first.

## Common defaults

These are not permanent bans. They are defaults that require a concrete reason:

- centered headline over a gradient hero;
- purple-blue neon as an unearned technology signal;
- glass cards, uniform radius, uniform shadows, and pill buttons;
- three or four equal cards as the whole information structure;
- generic icon grids and stock illustrations;
- every section repeating the same component family;
- every web element fading or sliding upward;
- random parallax, infinite floating, particles, and unmotivated zoom;
- every video shot using the same duration, easing, lens, or transition;
- placeholder copy, fake evidence, repeated low-resolution assets, and random stock imagery.

If a default remains, state:

```yaml
exception:
  premise_served: ""
  relationship_changed: ""
  memory_created: ""
  cost_accepted: ""
```

If it cannot answer these questions, remove it.

## Decision-first review output

Do not produce a long list of interchangeable improvements. Use:

```text
Blunt verdict:
Dominant failure:
Three located evidence points:
Highest-leverage change:
What to remove:
How the next proof will falsify or confirm the change:
```

“高级、现代、科技、电影感、统一、年轻、冲击力” are not evidence. Replace them with visible facts about position, scale, contrast, type behavior, material response, camera, timing, or interaction.

## Human rejection is useful data

When a user says “丑”, “普通”, “廉价”, “没感觉”, or “像模板”, do not argue for the direction. Preserve the phrase, classify the failure, and decide whether the thesis must change. If the same spine is rejected twice, stop polishing and replace it.
