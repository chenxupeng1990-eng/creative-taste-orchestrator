# Product light stage

Readiness: `production_spec`. This is a production method, not a shipped image set, model, renderer, or high-end quality benchmark.

Use for a product-led beauty or design hero when the product must feel present in a coherent environment. Prefer layer-led or hybrid production; use an existing approved visual when it already meets the brief. Do not use it to force every brand into white minimalism.

## Inputs and decisive output

Inputs: verified product/packaging reference, intended desktop/mobile crops, actual headline and copy, display scale, and an inspected light/material reference. The decisive output is one composited hero frame, then its short transition if needed—not a complete site of placeholders.

## Build order

1. Compose the product, reflection/shadow region, and type-safe space together. Determine subject-to-headline scale and camera angle before adding ornaments.
2. Create the environment plate or render scene. For a white environment, separate subject edges through controlled reflection, darker edge response, and local tonal differences rather than increasing bloom until edges disappear.
3. Light the real subject. A broad light source, a controlled darker reflection and an appropriately placed contact shadow are a starting experiment, not fixed values. Glass, liquid, label and closure must not all respond as one shiny plastic surface.
4. Composite or render with consistent perspective and lighting. Separate the product, environment, shadow/reflection, and optional refractive layer only when those parts need independent control. Preserve registration using [asset-production.md](../references/asset-production.md).
5. Put actual type over the composite. Inspect at intended size and on mobile, including label integrity, focal competition and copy wrapping. Do not fill the page with production commentary.
6. For motion, choose one purpose: reveal contour, change viewpoint, or hand attention to an ingredient detail. Move the light/object/camera relationship that causes the effect. Unrelated particles are not a substitute for material quality.

## Compare the cause

Before inventing another concept, compare the same composition with one coherent craft change: light size/angle, edge treatment, shadow placement, crop, or subject scale. Inspect the product both isolated and in the page. Preserve the strongest version with the existing comparison/application tools.

Failure signals: rectangular photo matte, disconnected contact shadow, clipped or invented label, flat bottle that does not fit the scene, highlights with no apparent light source, texture fighting the type, or desktop crop mechanically squeezed onto mobile. If the image is the failure, remake the image; do not add effects to conceal it.

## Completion of this recipe in a project

Keep source assets/composite or scene, generated outputs with actual receipts where applicable, desktop/mobile proof, any motion excerpt, and the specific selected edit. Record which aspects were inspected. A layout blockout can proceed provisionally, but it cannot establish that photo-real assets or a premium material pass have been delivered.
